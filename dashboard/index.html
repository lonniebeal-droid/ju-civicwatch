Georgia · statute

Collection, storage, sharing, and destruction of captured license plate data for law enforcement purposes; penalty

O.C.G.A. § 35-1-22

enactedALPRchecked 2026-11-06

Statewide rule for law-enforcement automated license plate readers. Enacted in 2018 by HB 79 and amended effective July 1, 2025 by SB 153. Access to captured plate data is limited to a law-enforcement purpose, the default destruction period is 30 months, agencies that deploy a system must keep use and training policies, and captured plate data is not an Open Records disclosure.

https://law.justia.com/codes/georgia/title-35/chapter-1/section-35-1-22/

Notes

Section text checked against the public 2025 Code of Georgia page. This is a statute, not a city ordinance. Official compilation PDF: https://www.legis.ga.gov/api/document/docs/default-source/joint-features-document-library/t35-t36-ch1-59-(v27)-2025-pdf.pdf
